Looker Studio blends join at query time, at whatever grain the chart asks for. The same join in Omni would fan out if the two sides were joined at row level. The plan's model route is `build`, so the join is made once in dbt at a stated daily grain, and Omni reads one table. Join type 2 is confirmed as a full outer join; a blend with an unconfirmed join code would be a `needs_human` item instead.
